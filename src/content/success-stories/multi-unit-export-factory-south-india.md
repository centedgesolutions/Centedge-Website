### Project Overview
A multi-unit apparel manufacturing facility supplying leading international brands required immediate workplace transformation to prepare for rigorous Tier-1 brand compliance audits.

### The Strategy
Cent Edge instituted our proven 5S Governance Framework:
- **Red-Tagging Days:** Cleared over 4 tonnes of obsolete machinery, scrap fabric, and disused fixtures.
- **Visual Factory Standards:** Color-coded floor demarcation for raw materials, WIP, and finished goods.
- **Tool Shadow Boards:** Standardized every sewing station with dedicated slots for snips, gauges, and oil cans.

### Sustainable Results
The factory passed international buyer audits with an 89% score (Platinum Level) and sustained the standard over subsequent quarters through peer-led weekly audits.
