### Project Overview
A premier woven garment manufacturer in Bangalore producing high-specification outerwear experienced severe production bottlenecks, long cycle times, and frequent airfreight penalties due to late deliveries. 

### The Diagnostic
Cent Edge consultants conducted an intensive 5-day on-floor diagnostic:
1. **Unbalanced Line Stations:** High cycle time variance between preparation and assembly stages.
2. **Hidden WIP:** More than 2.5 days of cut parts sitting in bundles between line sections.
3. **Motion Waste:** Operators losing up to 18% of productive time searching for trims and thread spools.

### The Implementation
- **Cellular Modular Layout:** Reconfigured linear batch lines into modular flow cells.
- **Hourly Target Boards:** Replaced retrospective daily reporting with live hourly pitch tracking.
- **Line Balancing:** Reallocated operations using GSD standard minute values to smooth flow.

### Measured Impact
Within 90 days of rollout, the unit documented sustained performance gains that directly impacted operating margins.
