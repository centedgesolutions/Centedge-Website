summary: >-
  Identified a primary manpower catchment within approximately 5–6 km of the
  proposed location.


  Established indicative monthly wage benchmarks of ₹12,000 for sewing operators and ₹8,000 for helpers.


  Highlighted safe, reliable transport and suitable working hours as critical enablers of workforce participation.


  Recommended a phased start with 300–400 machines, with a two-shift operating model to be evaluated against recruitment readiness and workforce preferences.


  The outcome
challenge: Eastman needed to establish the workforce feasibility of its proposed
  garment factory a. The investment decision required a clear understanding of
  available manpower, wage expectations, commuting needs and the scale of
  operations the local workforce could support.
solution: Cent Edge conducted a 10,000-household manpower survey to assess the
  labour catchment and translate the findings into factory planning inputs. The
  study examined worker availability, prevailing wages, travel preferences and
  barriers to participation, with particular attention to women’s safety and
  transport.
results: The project progressed to a successful factory launch, supported by a
  clearer understanding of the local workforce. The survey provided a practical
  foundation for recruitment, transport planning and capacity decisions, helping
  connect the investment plan with conditions on the ground.
